# Home Chef Companion Kit

Turn your AI into a personal **sous-chef** — technique, timing, flavor, equipment,
substitutions, measurements, pantry, meal planning, shopping lists, and food
safety, all adapted to **your** skill level, tastes, dietary pattern, equipment,
and pantry.

It's a portable text-file kit. Install it into the AI tool of your choice
(Muse AI, ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Cursor — anything
that reads long instructions), and your AI cooks alongside you: beginner to
pro, healthy whole-food cooking, zero judgment.

**What it is NOT:** not a diet advisor, not a clinician, not a cookbook (no
bundled recipes), not a calorie tracker. If a question crosses into medical or
nutrition-therapy territory, the companion says so and points you to a
professional.

## Who it's for

Anyone who cooks at home — from "I just learned how to hold a knife" to "I've
been smoking briskets for years." The companion meets you where you are, keeps
track of your preferences (fresh garlic vs. powder, tangy vs. sweet, what your
pantry actually holds), and grows with you. Shareable with family and friends.

## What's inside

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `AGENT.md` | The companion's persona: role, tone, boundaries, how it adapts to you |
| `skills/intake-and-adaptation/` | First-conversation intake: your skill level, tastes, dietary pattern, equipment, pantry — the guided "help me help you" quiz (or a conversational version) |
| `skills/kitchen-math/` | Conversions and scaling: cups to grams, doubling or halving recipes, pan sizes, temperatures |
| `skills/substitutions/` | Ingredient swaps: what's out, what's in the pantry, what works without wrecking the dish |
| `skills/food-safety/` | Safe internal temps, storage times, thawing, kitchen safety, and home food preservation |
| `skills/technique/` | Knife skills, searing, sautéing, resting, and the fundamentals that make everything better |
| `skills/cooking-methods/` | Grilling, smoking, roasting, braising, baking, pressure cooking — method guidance by method |
| `skills/diet-adaptation/` | Adapting recipes to dietary patterns (low-carb/keto, etc.) without losing the soul of the dish |
| `skills/meal-planning/` | Weekly meal plans and shopping lists, built around your store preferences and how you shop |
| `skills/pantry-tracker/` | Pantry and fridge inventory, restock reminders based on your cooking patterns, starter-pantry list for beginners |
| `skills/recipe-delivery/` | Recipes delivered three ways: on-screen, printable, or conversational walkthrough while you cook |
| `skills/recipe-storage/` | Saving and organizing your recipes (a personal Recipe Box) — what your platform can actually store |
| `templates/` | 8 blank fill-in templates: user profile, pantry inventory, meal plan, shopping list, recipe records, and more |
| `reference/` | Reference appendix: charts, tables, and quick-look data the companion consults when you ask — it doesn't get volunteered unprompted |

The kit tracks what matters to you — your preferences, your equipment, your
pantry — and cooks with that in mind every time. Nothing in the shipped files
is personal: your details live only in your own workspace, which is never
shared.

## Installation

Step-by-step guides for Muse AI, ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Microsoft
Copilot, Cursor/coding agents, and any other AI tool: see **INSTALL.md**.
Each guide states its platform's honest limits — what persists, what doesn't,
and where recipes can actually be saved.

**Quick version** — attach the kit's files to your AI chat, then send your AI
agent this message:

> Install this companion kit and start intake with me.

(The kit's public repo: https://github.com/dewers99/home-chef-companion-kit —
you can paste its link after the colon instead of attaching files.)

## Updates

The companion checks the repo's `VERSION` file — never applying anything
silently. The first check happens one week after install, then on your
schedule: weekly, monthly, or off. When an update exists, the companion shows
you exactly what changed and only then asks whether to apply it. Kits that
re-fetch from a URL each session are already current.

## Feedback

Two weeks after install, the companion will offer you an anonymous feedback
form — always skippable, never nagging, and not monitored (your responses are
only ever read by the kit's author, periodically).
