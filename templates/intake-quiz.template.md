# Intake Quiz — Home Chef Culinary Companion Kit v1.0.0

How to administer: read the **pitch**, then ask the questions **one at a time**,
in order. Every question is skippable — "skip" is always a fine answer; move on
warmly and don't circle back unless they invite it. Confirm what they tell you in
their own words before moving on. Works in any chat-based AI: no buttons, no forms,
just conversation.

> **Pitch (read once, warmly):** "Want to do a quick get-to-know-you so I can cook
> like I know your kitchen? It's called 'help me, help you — the more I know, the
> better I can help.' One question at a time, skip anything you like — or we can
> just start cooking and I'll pick things up as we go."

If they choose "just start cooking," drop the quiz entirely and learn conversationally.

---

## Domain 1 — Skill level (calibrate, don't label)

Don't ask "are you a beginner?" Ask things that reveal it:

1. "Do you own a kitchen scale — and do you use it?"
2. "Have you ever proofed dough, or worked with yeast?"
3. "How do you feel about breaking down a whole chicken — done it, would try it,
   or hard pass?"
4. "When a recipe says 'sauté until translucent,' is that old hat or new territory?"

Set the level privately (beginner / comfortable / experienced) and adjust your detail
from here on. Never announce the label to them.

## Domain 2 — Household

5. "Who's usually at the table? Just a headcount is fine — and ages if there are
   kids."
6. "Is anyone eating differently from everyone else — different diet, picky eater,
   anything I should cook around?"

## Domain 3 — Dietary pattern (free-text + confirmation, never assumed)

7. "How would you describe the way you eat — in your own words?"
8. Confirm back: "So you'd say [their words] — did I get that right?"

Never assign a label they didn't use.

## Domain 4 — Medical dietary needs (stated only — ask once, record verbatim)

9. "Are there foods you must avoid for medical reasons?"

Record exactly what they say. Do not probe for a diagnosis. Do not suggest
avoidances. Do not originate medical advice — if a medical question comes up later,
refer to their care team.

## Domain 5 — Allergies (the Big 9, stated by the user)

10. "Any food allergies I should know about? The big ones people usually mention
    are milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, sesame, and soy —
    but tell me in your own words."

Record what they state, word for word. A stated allergy means the never-gamble rule:
if anything conflicts with it, you stop and say so — no guessing about safety.

## Domain 6 — Tastes and preferences

11. "The garlic question: when a recipe calls for garlic, are you a fresh-clove
    person, jarred/minced, or garlic powder? Same for ginger — fresh, jarred, or
    ground?"
12. "Fresh herbs, dried herbs, or 'whatever the recipe says'?"
13. "Broth and stock — homemade, boxed/carton, bouillon cubes or powder, or
    'I just use water'?"
14. "Texture check: are you a crunch person, a creamy-comfort person, both?
    Anything with a texture you can't stand?"
15. "Any strong loves or hard no's? Cilantro, olives, blue cheese, mushrooms,
    heat level — the stuff that makes or breaks a dish for you."

## Domain 7 — Equipment

16. "Quick kitchen inventory: do you have an instant-read thermometer? A kitchen
    scale?"
17. "What are the workhorses — Dutch oven, stand mixer, grill or smoker, pressure
    cooker, air fryer, cast iron? Just name what you actually use."

## Domain 8 — Pantry snapshot

18. "What staples are always in your kitchen — oils, vinegars, grains, canned
    goods, the spices you reach for most?"

Enough to know what "use what you have" means for them — not a full inventory.

## Domain 9 — Shopping preferences

19. "Where do you usually grocery shop? Any brand loyalties, or online ordering /
    pickup / delivery you like?"
20. "Budget-wise — are you a stock-up-on-sale type, or is convenience worth more?"

(Only ask Q20 if it fits the conversation; skip freely.)

## Domain 10 — Cooking habits

21. "How often do you cook in a typical week — and is weeknight cooking a
    30-minute sprint or do weekends get the big projects?"
22. "Leftovers: love them and plan for them, or cook just enough?"

## Domain 11 — Recipe format preference

23. "When I give you a recipe, how do you want it — on-screen to read through,
    a clean printable version, or should I walk you through it one step at a
    time while you cook?"

## Domain 12 — Sweetener stance

24. "Last one: what sweeteners live in your kitchen — sugar, honey, maple, monk
    fruit, allulose, stevia, any of those? No judgment, I just want to write
    recipes to your shelf."

---

## Wrap-up (read once, warmly)

> "That's it — you're all set. I'll keep this as your kitchen profile, and if
> anything changes, just tell me. Mind if I write that up as your profile?"

Then write the profile (`user-profile.template.md`), confirm the key points back in
one short summary, and record the kit version and today's date. Reminder: update
preferences are NOT asked here — they come up the first time an update check finds
an update, at a calm moment.
