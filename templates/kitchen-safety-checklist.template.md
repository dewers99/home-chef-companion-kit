# Kitchen Safety Checklist

**Home Chef Companion Kit v1.0.0 — template.**
The companion helps the user fill this out **once** (a guided walk-through of their kitchen), then reminds at the user's chosen cadence. The user picks the cadence — monthly, quarterly, whatever they'll actually do.

> Fill it out for real — dates, not vibes. "Detector tested" means you pressed the button this month.

## Fire extinguisher

- [ ] **ABC-rated multipurpose dry-chemical extinguisher** in or near the kitchen (Class K is for commercial kitchens — homes need ABC)
- **Location:** (near the kitchen **exit path** — NEVER above or beside the stove)
- **Weight/size:** (e.g., 5 lb)
- [ ] One extinguisher on **every level** of the home
- [ ] **Last monthly inspection date:** — gauge in the green, pin intact, nozzle clear, no damage
- [ ] Everyone in the house knows **PASS** (Pull, Aim at the base, Squeeze, Sweep)
- [ ] **Reminder cadence:** monthly inspection on the ____ of each month
- **Recharge/replace after ANY use** — a used extinguisher is not a safety item

## Grease-fire steps posted

- [ ] Steps posted or kept where the cook will see them (fridge door, inside a cabinet door — not above the stove):
  1. **Slide a metal lid over the pan** (from the side, mitts on). Don't drop it.
  2. **Turn off the heat** — if you can do it safely and quickly.
  3. **Leave the lid on until the pan is completely cool.**
  4. Small and contained only: lots of **baking soda** (or salt).
  5. ABC extinguisher is a **last resort** — abandon instantly if it doesn't work.
  6. **NEVER water. NEVER move the pan. NEVER flour.**
  7. If it won't go out: **get out, call 911 from outside.**
- [ ] **Box of baking soda kept NEAR (not above) the stove** — accessible without reaching over flames
- [ ] No flour, baking powder, or look-alike powders stored within reach of the stovetop
- [ ] Oven mitts stored **away from the stovetop** (not hanging next to the burners)
- [ ] Small kitchen first-aid kit stocked: sterile dressings/gauze, adhesive bandages, antibiotic ointment, medical tape, scissors, disposable gloves

## Outdoor cooking (grill / smoker)

- [ ] Grill/smoker sits **well away from the house**, out from under eaves, clear of deck railings and overhanging branches
- [ ] Grease management on schedule — drip tray, grease channel, and grates cleaned per the manufacturer (grease buildup is the leading grill-fire ignition factor)
- [ ] **3-foot kid/pet zone** observed around the grill while lit
- [ ] Gas grill: **lid open BEFORE lighting**; propane connections checked for leaks (rotten-egg smell = get away, no switches, call 911 from a safe distance)
- [ ] Charcoal: coals disposed in a **metal container with a tight-fitting lid**, outside, away from combustibles
- [ ] Extinguisher within reach of the outdoor cooking area
- [ ] **Smoker fire plan:** turn unit off, lid closed, no water, call the fire department
