## Pre-use check (before opening any jar, every time)

- [ ] Lid tight, center concave — no bulging, no leaking, no dried-food streaks on the jar
- [ ] Contents look normal — no rising air bubbles, no unnatural color
- [ ] On opening: no unnatural odor, no spurting liquid, no cotton-like mold
- [ ] **When in doubt, throw it out. Never taste-test a questionable jar.**
- [ ] Suspect jar: still-sealed → heavy-bag to trash (no boiling); unsealed/open/leaking → 30-minute boil detox, then trash
